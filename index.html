About Jamhuriya University

Jamhuriya University Student Attendance is designed to help academic staff manage classroom presence, monitor student participation, and generate attendance performance records for the university community.

The system stores student records, tracks each day’s attendance status, and provides live dashboard insights for Present, Absent, and Late attendance.

This platform supports efficient monitoring of student engagement across all classes and keeps attendance records easy to review, update, and report.
